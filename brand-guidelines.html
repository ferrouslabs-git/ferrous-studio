01 — Brand essence

Clarity over hype.

Ferrous Labs is an AI transformation studio that builds and runs production AI for businesses. The brand voice is the antidote to AI hype: plain-spoken, technically credible, and relentlessly focused on outcomes that move a number on the client's P&L. The personality is "The Bold Alchemist" — confident and experimental, turning raw business problems into engineered systems.

Positioning

AI Transformation Studio. We build AI systems that improve productivity, enhance customer experiences, and enable new products and services.

Promise

We build and run the AI your business doesn't have.

Personality

Bold, precise, anti-hype. Engineer's rigour with a maker's confidence. Never salesy, never jargon for its own sake.

"Build a competitive advantage with AI."

Primary brand headline
02 — Logo

One mark, picked for contrast.

The Ferrous Labs logotype and standalone symbol ship in blue, white, and orange-accented variants. Choose the variant that holds the strongest contrast against its background. The brand is dark-first, so the white and white/orange marks are the defaults.

Full logotype
White / Orange — default on dark
White — mono on dark
Blue / Orange — on light
Blue — mono on light
Symbol (favicon / avatar)
White symbol — on dark
Blue symbol — on light / favicon

Do

  • Keep generous clear space — at least the height of the symbol on all sides
  • Use the white or white/orange mark on dark surfaces (the brand default)
  • Use SVG so the mark stays crisp at any size
  • Nav lockup sits around 28px tall; never below ~20px for legibility

Don't

  • Recolour, gradient-fill, or add effects to the mark
  • Stretch, rotate, or crop the logotype or symbol
  • Place a low-contrast variant on a busy or mid-tone background
  • Rebuild the wordmark in another typeface

04 — Typography

Two typefaces, clear jobs.

Space Grotesk carries all display and body text — headlines run tight with negative letter-spacing. IBM Plex Mono is the system voice: eyebrow labels, captions, tags, and buttons, always uppercase with wide tracking. The contrast between the two is itself a brand signature.

Display H1Space Grotesk · 600 · -0.03em · line 1.05
Heading H2Space Grotesk · 600 · -0.025em
Heading H3Space Grotesk · 600 · -0.01em

Eyebrow labelIBM Plex Mono · 0.16em · uppercase · orange

Button labelIBM Plex Mono · 0.12em · uppercase

Headline rhythm

Headlines often break across lines with intentional <br> rhythm and end on a short, punchy clause. Tight tracking (-0.03em) gives them a confident, engineered feel.

Web fonts

Space Grotesk 400/500/600 and IBM Plex Mono 400/500/600, loaded from Google Fonts. System fallback: system-ui, sans-serif / monospace.

05 — Visual signatures

The details that read as Ferrous.

A handful of recurring effects make the brand recognisable before a word is read. Use them with restraint — one or two per view.

Molten divider
Animated gradient rule (cobalt → violet → orange) with a soft glow. Used to separate major sections.

Background grid

Faint orange 80px grid behind hero and CTA sections.

Glow & grain

Soft radial glows (cobalt/orange/violet) on light sections, plus a fixed 4.5%-opacity film grain over the whole page.

Gradient text

competitive advantage

Reserve the iridescent gradient for one or two key words per page — never whole sentences.

07 — Voice & tone

Plain-spoken. Outcome-first.

We write the way a sharp technical co-founder talks: direct, confident, and free of hype. Short declarative sentences. Concrete numbers over adjectives. We name the business problem before we name the technology.

Do

  • Lead with the business outcome, then the tech
  • Use plain, specific language — "10× cheaper", "hours → under a minute"
  • Keep sentences short; let line breaks set the rhythm
  • Be candid about trade-offs and constraints
  • Use mono eyebrow labels to frame each section

Don't

  • Lean on hype words — "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "synergy"
  • Bury the point in jargon or vendor-speak
  • Over-promise or make claims without numbers behind them
  • Use exclamation marks or emoji in brand copy
  • Write long, hedging paragraphs

"AI can do a hundred things. Most of them won't move a number on your P&L. These four will."

On-brand: outcome-first, plain, a little contrarian.

09 — CTA conventions

Two calls to action. Always the same.

Consistency here matters more than variety — every page uses the same two CTAs with the same labels, so visitors learn them.
